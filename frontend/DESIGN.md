## Brand & Style

The design system embodies the intimacy, grandeur, and timeless reverence of a haute couture wedding celebration. It is crafted for discerning couples and guests who value understated luxury over ostentatious flair. The visual language blends high-editorial classicism with modern digital grace, delivering an emotive experience akin to unboxing a bespoke letterpress invitation on handmade cotton paper.

The aesthetic philosophy merges **Editorial Minimalism** with **Tactile Fine Art**:
- Generous breathing room (macro whitespace) evocative of curated art journals.
- Subtle botanical line illustrations that frame, rather than overpower, key milestones and narratives.
- Gilded accents applied with restraint, echoing micro-embossed hot-foil stamps rather than harsh metallic gradients.
- Fluid, lingering transitions (soft fade-ins, subtle parallax) that honor the emotional gravity of holy matrimony and celebration.

## Colors

The palette is anchored in warm, luminous neutrals reminiscent of antique parchment and raw silk, elevated by warm precious metals and muted organic undertones.

- **Primary (`#C5A059` - Champagne Gold Foil):** Serves as the signature accent. Used for elevated interactive states, hairline borders, monogram insignia, and active milestones. A lighter variant (`#E5C887`) provides subtle ambient highlights.
- **Secondary (`#8A9A86` - Soft Heirloom Sage):** A calming botanical tone reflecting eucalyptus, olive wreaths, and garden foliage. Applied to secondary tags, confirmation toasts, and organic visual markers.
- **Tertiary (`#D8A499` - Dusty Petal Rose):** An intimate, warm blush hue used sparingly for romantic highlights, sentimental callouts, countdown badges, and RSVP badges.
- **Neutral Primary (`#2B2625` - Deep Espresso Charcoal):** Replaces harsh pure black to provide readable, high-contrast typography with an organic warmth that complements paper-white surfaces.
- **Surface & Canvas (`#FAF8F5` Warm Ivory & `#F3EFEA` Cream Alabaster):** Forms the layered background foundation, preventing eye fatigue and cultivating a warm, tactile atmosphere.

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy orchestrates a classical editorial balance: dramatic, high-contrast transitional serifs juxtaposed against disciplined, light-to-medium geometric grotesques.

- **Playfair Display (Display & Headlines):** Imparts romantic prestige and theatricality. Used for the couple’s names, chapter markers, formal quotes, and section anchors. Italicized cuts are leveraged intentionally for emotional phrases ("The Wedding of", "Together with their families").
- **Montserrat (Body & Micro-Copy):** Ensures pristine legibility on hand-held mobile viewports across complex details (itineraries, maps, banking registries, gift addresses).
- **All-Caps Tracking:** Labels, dates, countdown unit indicators, and category headings must utilize generous letter-spacing (`0.12em` to `0.18em`) with uppercase transformations to evoke archival museum cataloguing.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is built upon an intentional, editorial single-to-twelve column grid designed to prioritize vertical narrative pacing:

- **Canvas Constraints:** Desktop content is confined to a luxury magazine max-width of `1080px`, preventing sprawling text lines and preserving cinematic focus. Mobile layouts respect a strict single-column stream designed for one-handed thumb interaction.
- **Rhythmic White Space:** Sections are divided by spacious vertical margins (`space-xl` to double `space-xl`), allowing each ritual step—from the couple's introduction to the RSVP questionnaire—to exist as an unhurried, standalone tableau.
- **Card Gaps:** Sub-elements, such as event timeline cards or photo gallery tiles, utilize `space-lg` to create a deliberate sense of framed separation.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy rejects intense dropshadows and heavy black elevations, relying instead on fine-line boundaries and ambient luminous warmth:

- **Atmospheric Diffusion:** Elevated surfaces use soft, long-spread ambient shadows tinted with warm sepia (`rgba(43, 38, 37, 0.05)`) with an offset blur of `24px` to create an illusion of floating vellum paper.
- **Hairline Framing:** Cards, modal dialogs, and image frames feature ultra-thin (`1px`) borders in pale gold (`#C5A059` at 25% opacity) or softened stone (`#2B2625` at 8% opacity).
- **Glass Vellum Layering:** Overlays and sticky audio controls make use of a frosted paper effect (`backdrop-filter: blur(12px)`) bound to a semi-translucent warm ivory base (`rgba(250, 248, 245, 0.88)`), creating tactile depth without harsh breaks in narrative continuity.

## Shapes

The design system adopts a **Soft (Level 1)** structural silhouette, evoking precision-cut stationery and deckled-edge paper rather than bubbly modern widgets:

- **Interactive Corners:** Buttons, input wrappers, and micro-badges carry a crisp `0.25rem` (4px) curvature, preserving architectural discipline.
- **Card Envelopes & Imagery:** Content cards, portrait frames, and map panels utilize `0.5rem` (`rounded-lg`), soft enough to feel hospitable while maintaining editorial dignity.
- **Curated Exceptions:** Circular elements are strictly reserved for audio player toggles, monogram crest medallions, and decorative floral seal emblems.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Foil Action:** Champagne gold gradient fill (`linear-gradient(135deg, #D4AF37 0%, #C5A059 100%)`) with warm espresso text (`#2B2625`), styled in uppercase Montserrat (`12px`, letter-spacing: `0.14em`). Interactive hover adds a soft gold outer bloom (`0 4px 20px rgba(197, 160, 89, 0.35)`).
- **Secondary / Ghost:** Transparent background framed by a `1px` gold foil outline (`#C5A059`). Hover transitions softly to translucent champagne tint (`rgba(197, 160, 89, 0.08)`).
- **Envelope Opener (Cover CTA):** Distinctive pill/wax-seal hybrid button with an embossed inner gold rim and subtle floating pulse.

### Cards & Stationery Panels
- Base surface uses Alabaster Cream (`#FBF9F6`) finished with a hairline border (`rgba(197, 160, 89, 0.2)`).
- Corner flourishes feature minimal vector botanical line art (single-stroke weight `0.75px`) placed gracefully at opposing corners.

### Form Inputs & RSVP Controls
- **Text Fields:** Underline-only or subtle inset boxes with border bottom `1px` solid (`rgba(43, 38, 37, 0.2)`). On focus, transition border smoothly to `#C5A059` accompanied by floating uppercase labels.
- **Checkboxes & Radios:** Custom diamond-shaped or fine-circle selectors. Checked state features a champagne gold solid fill with an ivory tick or center gem dot.

### Timeline & Itinerary Trackers
- A central vertical `1px` hairline in antique gold anchors the agenda.
- Event nodes are marked by delicate botanical leaf accents or micro-diamond markers, paired with Playfair Display time indications.

### Specialized Wedding Modules
- **Digital Gift & Registry Box:** Clean copyable account numbers with dedicated one-click toast feedback, finished in an ivory card accented with gold-stamped bank logos.
- **Audio Ambience Controller:** Floating mini-disc or discreet gold chime icon in the bottom corner with low-contrast glassmorphism backing and play/pause state indicator.
